**{{ page.authors }}**

This study uses Fast Geometric Ensembling to produce multiple neural re-rankers and compares ways to combine their outputs, including score averaging, rank fusion, and supervised learning-to-rank. Experiments on MS MARCO show gains in ranking quality from ensembling, with particularly strong results from supervised combination methods.
